Privacy policy: NSE Internal Audit – N.A. Remarks

Effective 19 November 2026.

The NSE Internal Audit – N.A. Remarks Chrome extension is an internal tool used by Shah Kapadia & Associates.

What it handles. The extension reads the remarks Excel file you choose to upload, and the N.A. clause rows on the NSE internal audit report page at inspection.nseindia.com.

Where it is kept. The remarks from your Excel, your chosen signing account and your fallback remark are saved in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer. They are not synced to your Google account.

What is sent. Nothing. The extension makes no network requests. It does not send your Excel, the portal page content or any other information to the developer or to anyone else. It does not use analytics or tracking, and it does not sell or share data.

What it changes. The extension only writes remarks into empty N.A. remark boxes on the portal page, and only after you press Fill. It never saves or submits the report.

Removing your data. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. Uploading a new Excel replaces the previous remarks.

Contact. Questions about this policy: it@theska.in.
